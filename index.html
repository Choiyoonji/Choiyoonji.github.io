About

I am an M.S. student in the Interactive Robotics Lab at Dongguk University, advised by Prof. Soo-Chul Lim. My research focuses on bimanual robot learning and control, with a particular emphasis on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and their application to real-world manipulation tasks.

Research Interests

  • Bimanual Robot Learning & Control
  • Vision-Language-Action Models
  • Robot Teleoperation
  • Mixture-of-Experts Architecture
